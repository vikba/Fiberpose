# Example image sources

The example images in this folder are third-party work taken from the
publications listed below. They keep the license of their source and are
**not** covered by the GPL-3.0-only license of the Fiberpose code. Images were
converted to PNG and some were cropped.

## References

1. N. A. Hotaling, K. Bharti, H. Kriel, C. G. Simon. *Dataset for the validation
   and use of DiameterJ an open source nanofiber diameter measurement tool.*
   Data in Brief 5, 13–22 (2015). <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2015.07.012>.
   License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
   Described in: N. A. Hotaling, K. Bharti, H. Kriel, C. G. Simon. *DiameterJ: A
   validated open source nanofiber diameter measurement tool.* Biomaterials 61,
   327–338 (2015). <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biomaterials.2015.05.015>.
2. J. Huling, A. Götz, N. Grabow, S. Illner. *GIFT: An ImageJ macro for automated
   fiber diameter quantification.* PLOS ONE 17(10), e0275528 (2022).
   <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0275528>.
   License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
3. A. Obermayer, S. Hainzl, U. Koller, D. Lang, B. Lamprecht, W. Stoiber. *A block
   staining method using ethanolic phosphotungstic acid for the visualisation of
   collagens in transmission electron microscopy.* PLOS ONE 21(2), e0339342
   (2026). <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0339342>.
   License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
4. P. Chavoshnejad, M. J. Razavi. *Effect of the Interfiber Bonding on the
   Mechanical Behavior of Electrospun Fibrous Mats.* Scientific Reports 10, 7709
   (2020). <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-64735-5>.
   License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
